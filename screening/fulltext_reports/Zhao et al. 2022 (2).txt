**Notes for synthesis phase:** This study provides one of the few spatially explicit, scenario-based quantifications of how forest road role (pathway vs. sink) differentially shifts catchment-scale IC values (+2% to +10% mean increase), making it a strong effect-direction anchor for RQ evidence mapping. Its distinctive contribution is the MGWR-derived spatial decomposition showing that flow length is the dominant mediating variable and that road effects on IC and convergence processes are constrained within a ~60–180 m threshold zone, directly relevant to road-buffer management guidelines.
