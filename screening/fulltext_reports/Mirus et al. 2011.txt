## Stage-2 gate evaluation — Mirus et al. (2011)

**Gate 1 (full text):** Pass. Full-text PDF (18 pp.) is available. (Mirus et al. 2011, 1)

**Gate 2 (Stage-1 inclusion confirmed by full text):** **Fail.**

The stated objective is to evaluate how boundary-value-problem complexity affects the simulated representation of runoff-generation mechanisms (subsurface stormflow, Horton, Dunne) across four catchments using the InHM model — not to analyze forest-road, skid-trail, or in-stream structure effects on hydrological connectivity.  (Mirus et al. 2011, 2) Block-B infrastructure appears only as incidental site description: the C3 catchment is "intersected along its downstream boundary by a forest road, which is drained by a culvert", and the road is referenced as a feature of a prior slope-stability study (Mirus et al. 2007), but it is neither a treatment variable nor a parameter varied in the reduced-case comparisons of this paper.  (Mirus et al. 2011, 4) Connectivity (structural or functional) is not operationalized as an outcome metric; the response variables are integrated discharge and distributed runoff patterns under different parameterization detail.

Because gates are sequential and Gate 2 fails on the first applicable string, later gates (3–5) are not evaluated.
