### Content-coding suggestions

- 002: `002-C1-RetentionDetention` (primary proposal — windrows act as discrete slope-scale sediment/flow barriers) OR `002-B3-Structures`. Rationale: windrows are engineered post-harvest residue piles whose effect is barrier-type retention/roughness.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 2)
- 003: `003-Modeling` — DTM-based scenario simulation with the IC index.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 5)
- 004: `004-A3-ConnectivityMetrics` — Index of Connectivity used as the core quantitative descriptor.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 3)
- 005: `005-SouthAmerica` — Biobío Region, Chile.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 3)
- 010: `010-A3-ConnectivityMetrics` — IC computation is the principal connectivity operationalization; secondary `010-A2-FlowPathDescriptors` is defensible because IC is built on upslope/downslope flow-path components.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 5–6)
- 020: `020-B3-Structures` (proposed for windrows-as-mitigation-structures); roads/skid trails are only mentioned as harvest context, not analyzed as variables, so `020-B1-Roads` and `020-B2-SkidSnigTrails` are **not** justified.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 4)
- 030: `030-C1-RetentionDetention` (primary) — windrows function as decentralized slope retention; `030-C2-InfiltrationDisconnection` is a defensible alternative given the explicit framing as disconnection of sediment flow paths.  (Sanhueza et al. 2025, 6)

### Synthesis role

**Study_Type_Synthesis_Role:** `primary`
**RQ5 vote-counting:** included
**Notes for synthesis phase:** Provides a quantitative effect of a decentralized mitigation measure on structural connectivity in a paired-catchment-like modeling setting: contour-aligned coverage of 21.9% produced a statistically significant IC reduction, whereas 13.6% coverage with weaker contour alignment did not — yielding a configuration-over-coverage anchor and a distance-decay length (≈50–60 m from drainage) usable as a placement-rule heuristic in the Maßnahmenkatalog (Type B area modifier / roughness). Effect is on sediment connectivity, not directly peak flow — appropriate caveat needed when feeding into RQ6 flood-retention synthesis.
