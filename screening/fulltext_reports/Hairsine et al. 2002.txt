## Stage 2 screening — Hairsine et al. (2002)

### Gate evaluation

**Gate 1 — Full text:** PASS. The 17-page PDF is fully retrievable and machine-readable.

**Gate 2 — Stage-1 inclusion confirmed:** PASS. Block-B infrastructure (snig tracks / skid trails plus cross-banks) is the explicit independent variable, and connectivity is operationalized as an outcome through the *volume-to-breakthrough* concept, which is defined as "the volume of runoff that may enter an area before a discharge is observed at the downslope boundary of that area" — i.e., the threshold for establishing surface connection from cross-bank outlet to stream. (Hairsine et al. 2002, 4) The stated objective is to predict track-to-stream linkage, not merely runoff or sediment yield.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 2–3)

**Gate 3 — Q-items (primary study; modeling with empirical input):**
- Q1 context reportable: Eden Forest Management Area, SE New South Wales, Australia; three 160 ha compartments on light-granite, red-granite, and metasediment lithologies; nine instrumented hillslopes across three age classes. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 3)
- Q2 design classifiable: Modeling study (probabilistic plume-length model) parameterized with rainfall-simulator field data and GIS-derived track/stream geometry. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 3)
- Q3 method reproducibly described: Governing equations (1)–(8), assumption set, distributional parameters of vbt5 (μ = 336 L, σ² = 35 600), and DGPS/GIS workflow are all reported. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 5) (Hairsine et al. 2002, 8)
- Q4 infrastructure characterized: Snig tracks ~5 m wide, ⊥-to-contour on slopes ≤ 30 %; cross-banks 5–6 m wide, ~0.5 m high; networks occupy 4–6 % of compartment area. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 3) (Hairsine et al. 2002, 8)
- Q5 connectivity operationalized: Plume mean length vs. available hillslope length (1:1 criterion) plus summed PDF of stream-delivered runoff volume. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 10)
- Q6 effect direction derivable: Rainfall intensity ↑ → connection probability ↑ (0.5 %, 2 %, 16 % of plumes exceed available hillslope at low/medium/high intensity, MS compartment); time since logging ↑ → connection ↓; shorter delivery-path landscapes (MS, dendritic) → connection probability differs systematically from granite compartments. PASS.  (Hairsine et al. 2002, 12) (Hairsine et al. 2002, 14)

### Note for the SLR record

This record is one of the anchor citations on the AquaSilva connectivity-axis (Hairsine 2002, listed under forest-road/skid-trail connectivity anchors). It is methodologically distinctive in that it bridges structural connectivity (track-network topology, intercross-bank vs. available hillslope length) with a probabilistic representation of functional connectivity (plume-length distribution conditional on rainfall intensity and stand age), and supplies generic design equations directly relevant to the Maßnahmenkatalog Bauweise "Querablauf / Wasserableiter" under a Type B (area modifier: infiltration capacity of GHA buffer) parameterization.
