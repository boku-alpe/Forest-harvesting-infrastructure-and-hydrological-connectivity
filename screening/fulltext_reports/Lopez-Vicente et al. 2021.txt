## Stage-2 Screening: López-Vicente et al. (2021)

**Recommendation: `006-Include-Stage2` AND `006-Include-Final`**

### Gate-by-gate assessment

**Gate 1 (Full text):** Pass. 17-page PDF available.

**Gate 2 (Stage-1 inclusion confirmed):** Pass. Block-B infrastructure is operationalized as the treatment variable: four sub-sites differ explicitly by presence/absence and dimensions of log erosion barriers (LEBs) — long LEBs (Pinet-1: 16.5 LEBs/ha, mean length 20.5 m), no PFP (Pinet-2, Pinet-4), short LEBs (Pinet-3: 21.2 LEBs/ha, mean length 5.8 m), plus unburnt control. (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 3) Connectivity is the primary outcome, computed via the Aggregated Index of Connectivity (AICv2) of López-Vicente et al. with explicit Dup/Ddn formulation.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 5)

**Gate 3 (Q-items, primary study):** Pass on all six.
- Q1 context: Mediterranean, eastern Spain (Pinet, Valencia), Cambisol/Leptosol, 22% mean slope, scrubland-dominated, Llutxent 2018 wildfire (3270 ha).  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 3)
- Q2 design: paired/parallel sub-site comparison with pre-fire baseline + two post-fire drone scenarios (5.5 and 13 months APFP); unburnt control.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 3)
- Q3 method reproducibility: documented SfM/LiDAR DEM workflow (Pix4D → LAStools → ArcGIS Pro → resample 0.1 m → sink-fill), RGB→PCT vegetation classification, AICv2 equations given.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 4–5)
- Q4 infrastructure characterized: LEB density, length, occupied area per ha quantified per sub-site.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 3)
- Q5 connectivity operationalized: AICv2 with weighting factors (Rt, RT, Ct, KP, S) — structural-functional hybrid index.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 5)
- Q6 effect direction derivable: Δconnectivity reported per sub-site; LEB sub-sites showed 36% lower SC increase than no-PFP sub-sites; long LEBs −32% / −45% vs short LEBs across scenarios.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 1)

**Gate 4 (sample/scope):** Pass. Five sub-sites, total intersected working-area ≈ 163,120 m², two repeated UAV surveys.

**Gate 5 (effect direction extractable):** Pass. Direction and magnitude both quantified (see Q6).

### Notes for the human screener

- The paper sits at the boundary of Block B and Block C: the LEBs are simultaneously the **infrastructure variable** (B3) and a **mitigation control** (C1/Cany). Both blocks should be retained — this is one of the few records where post-fire structural mitigation is treated as the manipulated infrastructure within a connectivity-anchored design.
- Forest roads exist in the landscape (3.6% of total surface) but are explicitly **excluded** from the analytical mask, so `020-B1-Roads` is **not** recommended despite their physical presence.  (Lopez-Vicente et al. 2021, 1)
- No exclusion-reason note required.
