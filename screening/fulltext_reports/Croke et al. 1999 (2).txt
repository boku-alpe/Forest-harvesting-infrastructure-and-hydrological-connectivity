### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Include-Stage2` AND `006-Include-Final`
**Exclusion-reason string:** n/a
**Confidence:** Medium-High. Methodology and effect-direction are unambiguous; the only interpretive risk is Q5 / Gate 2(b) — the paper operationalizes flow-path linkage mechanistically (distances, velocities, time-of-connection) but does not adopt "hydrological connectivity" as an explicit framing concept. Given Croke is an anchor author and the paper directly informs forest-road / skid-trail connectivity discourse (cited by Croke 2005 and downstream connectivity literature), inclusion is defensible.
**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Q5 — confirm whether mechanistic flow-path quantification (transport distance, time-of-connection) satisfies the AquaSilva operationalization threshold, or whether a formal connectivity metric/index is required.
- Gate 2(b) — confirm acceptance of pre-2000 work that pre-dates explicit connectivity terminology but provides foundational quantification of structural/functional linkage between forest infrastructure and drainage network.
