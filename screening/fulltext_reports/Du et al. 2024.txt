Per the sequential first-failure rule, the binding failure is **Gate 2 (b)**.

### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Exclude-Quality`
**Exclusion-reason string (verbatim):** `connectivity only narrative, not operationalized`
**Confidence:** Medium-High. The exclusion logic is robust because the paper's outcome space is mapping accuracy, not connectivity. The Medium component reflects the borderline character: ditches *are* a Block-B infrastructure type, the paper *does* discuss hydrological connectivity conceptually, and a screener could plausibly argue Q5 as a pass given that the "minimum-cost connection" step is a structural-connectivity operation. If the human screener wishes to retain the paper as a methodological anchor for ditch-network extraction (rather than as effect evidence), that would be a defensible alternative outcome and should be documented as such.

**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Q5 (connectivity operationalization): consider whether the minimum-cost + FA connection procedure qualifies as a structural-connectivity operationalization in the A2-FlowPathDescriptors sense, even though no connectivity index is reported.
- Gate 2 (b): re-verify against the Stage 1 inclusion rationale; if Stage 1 admitted this paper specifically as a methods/mapping anchor, the human may prefer to record it as an out-of-scope methodological reference rather than as a Gate-2 quality exclusion.
- Topical fit: agricultural drainage in a predominantly cropland watershed — limited transferability to AquaSilva's forest-infrastructure focus in the Wienerwald pilot.
