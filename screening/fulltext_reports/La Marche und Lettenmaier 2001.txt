## Stage-2 Screening — La Marche & Lettenmaier (2001)

### Gate assessment

**Gate 1 — Full text retrievable:** Pass. PDF (20 pp.) is accessible. (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 1)

**Gate 2 — Stage-1 inclusion confirmed:** Pass. Block-B infrastructure (forest roads, culverts, road–ditch network of 547 km / 2171 culverts) is the explanatory variable, and connectivity is explicitly operationalized: culverts are classified as connected vs. non-connected via direct stream crossing or gully extension, and a logistic regression on hillslope curvature and downslope distance predicts gully connectivity (Pg threshold 0.5).  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 4–5) (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 8–9)

**Gate 3 — Q-items (primary study):**
- Q1 study context (Deschutes River 149 km², transient snow zone, marine Mediterranean climate, western Cascades): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 2–3)
- Q2 study design (combined field monitoring + distributed hydrologic modeling with DHSVM): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 1) (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 9)
- Q3 reproducible methods (crest-stage recorders at 21 segments; 530 km road survey; GIS/DEM connectivity model with explicit regression equation; DHSVM described with road/stream network coupling): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 4) (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 8–9)
- Q4 infrastructure characterized (road length, cutslope height classes, ditch-relief vs. stream-crossing culverts, contributing area): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 5–6)
- Q5 connectivity operationalized (four-category typology a–d; predictive logistic model; 24% of 1447 ditch-relief culverts modeled as connected): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 4–5) (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 9)
- Q6 effect direction derivable (quantified: +2.2–9.5% mean annual flood and +2.9–12.2% 10-yr event due to roads alone): Pass.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 1)

**Gate 4 — Sample/scope:** Pass. Nine subcatchments (2.2–21 km²) plus 149 km² main stem, two winter monitoring seasons (1996/97, 1997/98), 2171 surveyed culverts.  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 1) (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 5)

**Gate 5 — Effect direction extractable:** Pass. Direction is positive (road network increases peak flows; effect increases with return period).  (La Marche and Lettenmaier 2001, 1)
