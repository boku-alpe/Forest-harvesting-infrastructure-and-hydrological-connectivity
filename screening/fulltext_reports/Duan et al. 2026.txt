### Notes for the screener

This is a catchment-scale network analysis of dam/weir-induced river fragmentation for ecological (fish dispersal) restoration prioritization in a Chinese mountainous catchment. It is methodologically rigorous (Modeling + RF/GLMM) and operationalizes longitudinal connectivity quantitatively, but the connectivity construct is the Cote/Baldan/Rodeles dendritic-connectivity family (passability of in-stream barriers for biota), not the structural/functional hydrological connectivity of hillslope–channel flow paths relevant to forest-road retention. It therefore does not contribute to RQ1–RQ6 of the AquaSilva SLR and should not be carried into the corpus, consistent with the connectivity-anchored scoping rule.

No content-coding or synthesis-role block is generated, as the record is excluded at Gate 2.
