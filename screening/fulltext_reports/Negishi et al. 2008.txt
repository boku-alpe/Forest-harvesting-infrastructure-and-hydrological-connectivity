### Gate-by-gate appraisal

**Gate 1 — Full-text retrieval.** PDF accessible (18 pp.). Pass.

**Gate 2 — Confirmation of Stage-1 inclusion.** Block B infrastructure (a 51.5 m logging-road section, plus 690 m road network with skid trails and landings in catchment C3) is the central manipulated variable, not background context. (Negishi et al. 2008, 3) Connectivity is operationalized as flow-path interception: the road cut excavated into saprolite/bedrock truncates a hillslope (0.42 ha) containing a nested zero-order basin (0.14 ha), converting shallow subsurface flow into surface runoff that is routed via two discharge nodes through gullies directly into the stream.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 4) Pass.

**Gate 3 — Primary-study Q-items.**
- Q1 (context): Bukit Tarek Experimental Watershed, Peninsular Malaysia (3°31′N, 101°35′E); MAP ≈ 2862 mm; tropical headwater; slopes 40–45 %; 1-yr monitoring 24 Nov 2002 – 23 Nov 2003.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 2)
- Q2 (design): paired-/single-catchment field monitoring with event-based hydrograph and sediment sampling at three nested scales (road weirs, ZOB weir, catchment outlets C3 and undisturbed reference C1).  (Negishi et al. 2008, 4)
- Q3 (method reproducibility): v-notch weirs, WT-HR loggers at 2–3 min, ISCO automatic samplers, turbidity calibration, hydrograph separation per Negishi (2005), HOF/ISSF separation procedure explicitly described.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 4–6)
- Q4 (infrastructure characterization): road geometry (length 51.5 m, width 3.6 m, area 183 m², gradient 11.4 %), surface composition (subsoil/saprolite/bedrock fractions), Ks < 2 mm h⁻¹, bulk density > 1.58 Mg m⁻³, age 3 yr.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 3–4)
- Q5 (connectivity operationalized): explicit quantitative separation of ISSF into ISSF_hillslope (planar cutslope) and ISSF_ZOB (convergent zero-order basin), plus routing to stream via gullies.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 5)
- Q6 (effect direction): ISSF reaches >90 % of total road runoff in large storms; on average 79 % of runoff and 28 % of sediment export from the road section attributable to ISSF; road-surface sediment yield 170 t ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹; catchment suspended yield 4 t ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹.  (Negishi et al. 2008, 1) Pass.

**Gate 4 — Sample/scope.** 132 storms over 1 yr; 11 event-based sediment campaigns; paired reference catchment. Sufficient. Pass.

**Gate 5 — Effect direction extractable.** Quantitative directional effects reported with units (see Q6). Pass.
