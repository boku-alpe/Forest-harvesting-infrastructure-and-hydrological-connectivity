# Stage 2 screening — Martínez-Murillo & López-Vicente (2018)

## Gate assessment

**Gate 1 — Full-text retrievable:** PASS. Full PDF accessible (12 pages). (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 1)

**Gate 2 — Stage-1 inclusion confirmed:** PASS. Block-B infrastructure is operationalized as a model variable across eight scenarios — salvage-logging skid trails, 23 check dams (12 wood, 11 concrete), and linear landscape elements (roads, trails, streets) are explicitly varied between scenarios.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 704) (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 705) Connectivity is the explicit outcome, computed as the Borselli/Cavalli IC index.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 704)

**Gate 3 — Q-items (primary study, modeling with field validation):**
- Q1 context: Mediterranean Spain, 330 ha, 11 sub-catchments, MAP 600 mm, slopes >30%, Leptosols/Regosols, parent material marbles — reportable.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 703)
- Q2 design: GIS-based connectivity modeling with photo-interpretation and field-survey validation — classifiable as Modeling.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 706)
- Q3 method reproducibility: IC formulation, D-infinity flow routing, 2.5 m LiDAR DEM, W-factor parameterization with burn-severity weights (1.10/2.25/3.75/5.25) all reported.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 704) (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 706)
- Q4 infrastructure characterized: skid-trail emergence, 23 check dams with siltation states (empty / partially / fully silted), LLE composition documented.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 705)
- Q5 connectivity operationalized: IC mean, SD, and δ-values per scenario tabulated.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 708)
- Q6 effect direction derivable: explicit signed percentage changes per sub-catchment type.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 707)
All Q-items pass.

**Gate 4 — Sample/scope:** PASS. 11 sub-catchments × 8 scenarios with both within-burned-area and whole-catchment aggregation.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 707–708)

**Gate 5 — Effect direction extractable:** PASS. Skid trails + salvage logging raise IC by +18.2 % in forestry-with-check-dam sub-catchments and by +43.2 % within the LLE themselves; check dams produce a short-distance reduction insufficient to reverse the overall trend; vegetation recovery progressively lowers IC.  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 707–708) (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 710)

## Note for AquaSilva relevance

This record is methodologically aligned with the connectivity-anchored corpus (Borselli/Cavalli IC, D-infinity, explicit structural/functional distinction following Turnbull et al. 2008 and Wainwright et al. 2011).  (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 701) (Martínez-Murillo and López-Vicente 2018, 704) Although the setting is post-fire Mediterranean rather than Wienerwald forest hydrology, the check-dam parameterization via stream-mask updates with siltation states is directly transferable to Type-A discrete-storage and Type-C channel-modifier coding in AquaSilva_Katalog_v4.
