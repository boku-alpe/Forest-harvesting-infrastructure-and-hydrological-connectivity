### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Exclude-Quality`
**Exclusion-reason string:** `connectivity only narrative, not operationalized`
**Confidence:** Medium
**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Gate 2(b) / Q5: Borderline. The paper *does* quantify sediment flux along the road-prism delivery pathway (source → table-drain → drainage outlet), which could be read as a *functional connectivity within the road prism* operationalization, even though the term is absent and the analysis stops at the drain outlet (no road-to-stream link quantified here — that is the companion paper Croke et al. 2005). Recommend the screener decides whether sediment-redistribution-along-pathway evidence (Tables III, Figures 8–9) is sufficient to meet the AquaSilva connectivity-anchored threshold, or whether this paper should instead be retained only as a supporting cite for the Croke et al. 2005 connectivity paper.
- If the screener reclassifies Gate 2(b) as PASS on the "functional connectivity along delivery pathway" reading, all other Q-items pass and the paper would flip to `006-Include-Stage2` + `006-Include-Final`.
