### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Exclude-Quality`
**Exclusion-reason string (verbatim):** `connectivity only narrative, not operationalized`
**Confidence:** Medium

**Rationale (decision-support, not authority):** The paper is methodologically sound and operationalizes forest-road infrastructure rigorously, but the outcome variable is soil organic carbon loss; hydrological/sediment connectivity is invoked only as a discursive explanation in the Discussion ("higher sediment connectivity" in G flow paths) without an operationalized connectivity metric or mechanistic quantification. Per the connectivity-anchored SLR scope (and the explicit Type-I-error guard against pure runoff/erosion-hydrology studies), Gate 2(b) is the first failure.

**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Gate 2(b) borderline call. The flow-path typology (G / GD / D) and morphometric descriptors (W/D, Dr, Csa) are *conceptually adjacent* to A2-FlowPathDescriptors, and the paper cites two anchor sources (Takken 2008; Croke & Hairsine 2006). A human screener may wish to verify whether the AquaSilva protocol treats quantified flow-path morphology on road-stream slopes as a sufficient connectivity operationalization, or whether an explicit connectivity index/IC-type metric is required. If the broader reading is adopted, this paper would flip to `006-Include-Stage2`.
- Q5 (interpretive item) follows the same logic as Gate 2(b) and should be revisited together.
