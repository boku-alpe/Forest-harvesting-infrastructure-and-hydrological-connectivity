### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Exclude-Quality`
**Exclusion-reason string (verbatim):** `connectivity only narrative, not operationalized`
**Confidence:** Medium
**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Gate 2(b) is the principal judgment call. A screener could argue that Cl (drainage area per unit road length) plus the quantified terrace-induced reduction of effective contributing area to roads (Cl drops from 143.56 to 34.06 m² m⁻¹; road erosion −85 % under terraces  (Wang et al. 2022, 6)) functions as a functional-connectivity proxy (hillslope→road disconnection by structural BMPs). If the protocol admits implicit-connectivity proxies, this paper could be reclassified as a borderline Include with `004-A2-FlowPathDescriptors` and `030-C1-RetentionDetention` / `030-C3-RoadDrainageBMP` coding.
- The framing is forest-road / cropland-road erosion in an agricultural-forest watershed (forest cover 52 %, roads draining mixed land use), so it sits at the edge of the Block-B "forest road / skid trail" anchor literature (Wemple, Croke, Takken). Screener should decide whether the agricultural-road context disqualifies it independently of Gate 2.
