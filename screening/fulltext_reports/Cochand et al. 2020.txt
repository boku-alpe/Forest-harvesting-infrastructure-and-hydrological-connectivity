### Synthesis role

**Study_Type_Synthesis_Role:** `primary`
**RQ5 vote-counting:** included
**Notes for synthesis phase:** Strongest contribution is a quantitative effect-size anchor for **road-drainage design effect on subsurface connectivity**: L-drain induces ~10× flow concentration at the outlet and ~10× depletion in adjacent sections relative to base case, while distributed-permeability designs (wood-log, no-excavation) preserve near-natural flow fields  (Cochand et al. 2020, 11). Useful for the Maßnahmenkatalog as a Type C (channel/cross-section modifier) cautionary anchor: concentrated point-discharge culvert/L-drain outlets generate the cascade trigger (threshold → fill → overflow → new flow path) the AquaSilva Durchlass/Überlauf column groups are designed to capture. Domain (sloping fens, not forest hillslopes) should be reported with the effect size in RQ6.
