**Note for screener:** The paper has high methodological quality and explicitly invokes "connectivity of sediment transport pathways" as a keyword and conceptual frame  (Wilson et al. 2001, 1) (Wilson et al. 2001, 15), which may have driven Stage-1 inclusion. However, the connectivity that is modelled is *post-fire hillslope-to-stream sediment connectivity controlled by burn severity and cover* — there is no analysis of forest roads, skid trails, culverts, or BMPs as connectivity-modifying features. If retained anywhere, it could serve as a **methodological reference** for flow-path-based sediment routing in Raven/ZEMOKOST parameterization discussions, but not as evidence in the connectivity-anchored corpus.
