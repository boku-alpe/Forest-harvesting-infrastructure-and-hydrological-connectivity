### Recommendation

**Terminal tag:** `006-Exclude-Quality`
**Exclusion-reason string (verbatim):** `connectivity only narrative, not operationalized`
**Confidence:** Medium-High. The paper is a methodologically solid sediment-fingerprinting field study and does include unpaved roads as an analyzed source (Block B satisfied). However, hydrological connectivity is used exclusively as an interpretive narrative for spatial patterns in source apportionment; no structural or functional connectivity metric, flow-path descriptor, or mechanistic quantification is reported. Under the connectivity-anchored PSALSAR scope, this places it outside the core corpus.
**Items flagged for human re-read:**
- Gate 2(b) / Q5 — confirm that the discussion-level invocation of "runoff connectivity" (p. 2249) is not backed elsewhere by a connectivity index or sediment delivery ratio (SDR = 0.71 % is reported on p. 2249 — screener may wish to judge whether SDR counts as a connectivity-related operationalisation; under a strict reading it is a sediment yield ratio, not a connectivity metric).
- Q6 — whether the proximity-dependent contribution of unpaved roads to fine sediment (p. 2248) can be extracted as an indirect functional-connectivity signal for RQ6 context, even if the paper is excluded from the core corpus.

If the screener decides the SDR + proximity-gradient evidence is sufficient to upgrade Gate 2(b) to PASS, the paper would otherwise meet Q1–Q4 and Q7, and could be re-routed to `006-Include-Stage2` with content coding `002-B1-Roads`, `003-FieldMonitoring`, `004-RunoffResponse`, `005-SouthAmerica`, `020-B1-Roads`. This is flagged as a judgment call, not a unilateral recommendation.
