**Notes for synthesis phase:** This paper provides one of the most mechanistically explicit field demonstrations that road cutbank depth relative to soil depth is the key site characteristic controlling hydrological connectivity between hillslope subsurface flow and the stream network; the *h*rc metric (eq. 10) is directly extractable as a replicable connectivity index. It also provides a rare subcatchment-scale partitioning of road runoff between surface and subsurface sources (>95% from subsurface interception in this steep, permeable-soil setting), making it a key anchor for any RQ on the mechanisms of road-induced connectivity increase.
