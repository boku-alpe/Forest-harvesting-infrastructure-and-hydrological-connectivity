**Notes for synthesis phase:** The paper's most distinctive contribution is its empirical, connection-type-resolved quantification of how road–channel hydrological connectivity amplifies erosion by erosion category and road function type (Upslope-Road connections driving +200% landslide erosion on petroleum roads and +99% collapse erosion on agricultural roads), providing one of the few field-monitored datasets distinguishing structural from functional connectivity effects under a single extreme event.
