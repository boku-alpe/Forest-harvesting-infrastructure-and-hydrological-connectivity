### Synthesis role

**Study_Type_Synthesis_Role:** `primary`
**RQ5 vote-counting:** included
**Notes for synthesis phase:** Provides one of the early quantitative anchors for skid-trail → cross-bank → hillslope sediment disconnection (delivery 15–40 %, storage 50–85 %, >50 % of fines deposited within 55 m). Useful as RQ6 effectiveness datapoint for `C3-RoadDrainageBMP` (cross-bank / water-bar style runoff diversion) in temperate eucalypt forest on steep (25–30 %) granite and metasediment terrain, and as a methodological precedent for using sediment delivery ratio plus flow-path length as a functional-connectivity proxy at the hillslope scale.
