**Notes for synthesis phase:** The paper's most distinctive contribution is a low-cost, scalable field method for directly quantifying *functional* connectivity (stick+gauze trace height) at road–channel intersections, applicable across 137 connection points over three years — substantially larger spatial coverage than most plot-level connectivity studies. The key extractable finding for vote-counting is a strong positive effect of road networks on hydrological connectivity activation, with intensity scaling non-linearly with rainfall magnitude (degree 4.51× higher under LH-R vs. SL-R for upslope–road connections), and road-type moderating the direction of the effect (agricultural roads amplify upslope–road connectivity; petroleum roads amplify road–downslope connectivity).  (Xu et al. 2024) (Xu et al. 2024)
