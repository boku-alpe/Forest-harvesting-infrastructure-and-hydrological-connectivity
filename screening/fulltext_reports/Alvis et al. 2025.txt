**Notes for synthesis phase:** Strongest contribution is a quantitative within-road-segment effect: rut incision past a threshold depth measurably alters surface flow networks and increases relative sediment transport potential, with explicit drainage metrics (CMa, Ra, εi) suitable as anchors for A2/A3 operationalization of structural connectivity on the road prism.
