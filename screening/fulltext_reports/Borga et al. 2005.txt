### Content-coding suggestions

- **002:** `002-B1-Roads` — Forest roads are the sole infrastructure class modeled and surveyed.  (Borga et al. 2005, 1)
- **003:** `003-Modeling` — Distributed hydro-geomechanical model is the primary methodology; field data used for evaluation, not for paired-catchment or experimental design.  (Borga et al. 2005, 13)
- **004:** `004-A2-FlowPathDescriptors` — Core focus is alteration of subsurface flow paths by road interception/rerouting, quantified per pixel via λ1/λ2 partitioning of contributing area.  (Borga et al. 2005, 5)
- **005:** `005-Europe` — All four study areas in north-eastern Italy (Trentino-Alto Adige and Euganean Hills).  (Borga et al. 2005, 6)
- **010:** `010-A2-FlowPathDescriptors` — Block-A coverage centers on flow-path topology and rerouting rather than connectivity indices or pure concept work.  (Borga et al. 2005, 3)
- **020:** `020-B1-Roads` — Forest roads (cut-and-fill, with cross-road ditches and culverts) are the Block-B element.  (Borga et al. 2005, 7)
- **030:** `030-empty` — No mitigation/BMP intervention is tested; the paper only notes in the conclusions that the approach "may be useful for defining criteria for road design", which is not an evaluated mitigation.  (Borga et al. 2005, 1)

Optional human consideration: if the screener treats culverts and cross-road ditches as a distinct B3 element (cf. Wemple/Croke convention), a second tag `020-B3-Structures` could be defended on the basis of the explicit cross-ditch / relief-culvert representation. Proposal only; not assigned.

**Notes for synthesis phase:** Strongest contribution is a mechanistic, GIS-distributed operationalization of road-induced flow-path rerouting (λ1/λ2 partition of contributing area, RI statistic) with cross-site validation, suitable as an RQ1/RQ2 anchor for structural→functional connectivity translation and as a quantitative reference for RQ6 effect direction (road-induced increases in downslope wetness and landslide susceptibility, with site-dependent magnitude and occasional negative RI where roads divert flow away).
