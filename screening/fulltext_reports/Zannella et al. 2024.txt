### Rationale note for screener

The paper is methodologically strong (paired design, n = 25 pairs, GLMM, well-characterized catchments, reproducible analytical protocols) and would easily pass Q1–Q7 if connectivity were operationalized. It is a borderline-relevant **runoff-chemistry** study on **ditches** (Block-B), but per the AquaSilva connectivity-anchored protocol, pure runoff-hydrology/water-chemistry studies without explicit connectivity framing are excluded to avoid Type I errors. The phrase "deeper groundwater flow paths" is repeatedly used as a qualitative mechanism but is not measured (no GWT data, no flow-path tracing, no SC/FC metric). This is the canonical Gate 2(b) failure mode.

The paper may still be useful as **secondary context** for the Maßnahmenkatalog (ditch-cleaning side-effects on water quality and GHG), but it does not belong in the connectivity-anchored synthesis corpus.
