### Synthesis role

**Study_Type_Synthesis_Role:** `primary`
**RQ5 vote-counting:** included
**Notes for synthesis phase:** Strongest contribution is a *mechanistic structural-connectivity formulation* — explicit decomposition of road-influenced contributing area into upslope-of-road (A1), road-intercepted-and-rerouted (A2), and downslope-of-road (A3) components, plus the RI index — directly usable as a conceptual anchor for AquaSilva's structural-vs-functional connectivity distinction. The methodological finding that flow-routing algorithm choice (D8/MF/DINF) materially changes the spatial extent and magnitude of road-induced connectivity is a transferable caveat for ZEMOKOST/Raven preparation in the Wienerwald. Note the *outcome* is landslide hazard, not flood-peak attenuation; cite for RQ1–RQ2 (connectivity concepts / flow-path descriptors) rather than RQ6 (mitigation effectiveness on flood retention).
