### Note for screener

The paper is methodologically strong and would be a high-quality entry in a runoff-hydrology corpus, but the AquaSilva SLR is connectivity-anchored and requires Block-B infrastructure as a study variable. Dung et al. (2012) deliberately isolated the *vegetation-removal* signal from the *infrastructure* signal — making it the inverse of what the connectivity corpus targets. It may, however, be useful as a **contextual/contrast reference** in narrative synthesis (RQ6 framing: thinning without roads ≠ harvesting with roads), and could be retained outside the core corpus with a `99-context-reference` style tag if the screener wishes (proposal only, not a unilateral assignment).
