### Synthesis role

**Study_Type_Synthesis_Role:** `primary`
**RQ5 vote-counting:** included
**Notes for synthesis phase:** Strong anchor for the road–stream-crossing-as-connectivity-control hypothesis in a paired-catchment design (Wemple/Croke lineage, Southern Hemisphere). Contributes a quantitative effect-direction datapoint (persistent turbidity increase confined to the catchment with ≥5 piped stream crossings; <22 NTU mean differences) and an effectiveness signal for BMPs (riparian buffers + snig-track drainage + rapid revegetation can yield *net reductions* in turbidity post-harvest when permanent roads are absent). Useful for RQ6 as a long-record (15 yr) empirical reference, and as a counter-example showing connectivity dominance over harvest-disturbance intensity.
